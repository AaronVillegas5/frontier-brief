■ Two Steps Ahead

The next phase of AI will be defined by 'agentic reliability.' As models like GPT-6 Astra become more capable, the primary challenge for business owners will shift from 'what can the AI do?' to 'how do I ensure the AI does it correctly every time?' We are seeing a surge in 'harness' and 'proxy' technologies—tools designed to wrap around LLMs to provide security, memory, and error-checking.

Expect to see a move away from general-purpose chatbots toward specialized, 'hardened' agents that are integrated directly into enterprise software. The winners in this space will not necessarily be those with the largest models, but those who build the most robust 'guardrails' that allow these models to operate in high-stakes business environments without hallucinating or breaking critical workflows.
